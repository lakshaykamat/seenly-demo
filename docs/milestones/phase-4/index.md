# Phase 4: Intelligence & Action

> Turn scores into something users can act on. Multi-project support, scoped analysis, and the data flowing in and out.
>
> **Timeline:** 4 weeks
>
> **Depends on:** Phase 2 (runs, scores, evidence) and Phase 3 (billing, notifications)

After Phase 2, users have scores. After Phase 3, they're paying for them. Phase 4 is what makes those scores worth paying for — recommendations that say what to fix, project context that makes analysis meaningful, and data that can get in and out.

## Deliverable

Users see exactly what's dragging their score down, which source ecosystems are causing it, a rough score impact estimate, and a content draft to act on immediately. They can set up projects with market and language context so analysis is scoped to their business. They can see how visibility varies between France and the US, which competitors are gaining ground in AI responses, and what share of AI citations those competitors are capturing. Data comes in via CSV and goes out via CSV and a read-only API. The navigation makes the product make sense.
